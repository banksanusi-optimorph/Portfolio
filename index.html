Hi, I'm Funmilayo, a Business Operations and Transformation Consultant.

I help startups and organizations move from operational chaos to structured, scalable systems by designing and implementing the workflows, tools, processes, and teams that make businesses run better.

My work sits at the intersection of business operations, technology, automation, and strategy. I've built operational systems from scratch, redesigned processes, automated repetitive tasks, worked with ERPs and SaaS platforms, and connected systems to improve how businesses operate.

I work across ERP and SaaS platforms, APIs, process mapping, and business automation using tools such as Make.com, Google Apps Script, n8n, Zapier, Draw.io, and spreadsheets, while developing deeper capabilities in Python, JSON, SQL, and systems integration.

In simple terms, I help businesses figure out how work should happen, then build the systems that make it happen. If you need someone who understands both the business problem and the systems required to solve it, let's talk.

Core Philosophy

Strategy without execution is just ambition. Every transformation I lead is anchored in measurable outcomes and structured delivery.
